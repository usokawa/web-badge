Web Badge Studio User Manual

Navigation

  • workspace_premiumSwitch to the panel for controlling the digital pin badge.
  • photo_librarySwitch to the media editor panel to modify images, videos, and audio.

Badge Control

  • bluetoothConnect or disconnect your device and the digital pin badge via Bluetooth.
  • bug_reportToggle the visibility of the developer debug log.
  • languageChange the language setting of the digital pin badge to the selected language.
  • e911_emergencyCall the digital pin badge.
  • syncCheck and update the available storage space of the digital pin badge.

Media Editor

  • folder_openOpen a media file (image, video, or audio) to load into the app.
  • cropExtract the media within the preview frame to create images or audio.
  • aspect_ratioSelect the target resolution to match the screen size of the digital pin badge.
  • high_qualitySpecify the image quality (JPEG quality) for saving images.
  • grid_offDisable image smoothing to ensure pixel art remains crisp and clear without blurring.
  • rotate_rightAdjust the rotation angle of the loaded image or video.

Trim Panel

  • play_arrowPlay or pause the loaded video or audio.
  • startSpecify the exact start time (in seconds) for media extraction.
  • arrow_rangeSpecify the length of the clip (in seconds) to extract from the start time.
  • horizontal_align_rightSpecify the total duration of the audio track. If the duration exceeds the clip length, silence will be added to fill the gap. Set the duration to 0 and extract to delete the audio data.

Asset Dock

  • library_addAdd pre-made JPEG images or WAV audio files directly.
  • music_offSwitch to the audio track editing mode. The icon will change when audio data is added.

Status Bar

  • battery_fullDisplay the current battery level of the digital pin badge.
  • aspect_ratioDisplay the screen resolution of the digital pin badge.
  • storageDisplay the available storage space of the digital pin badge.

Preview & Upload

  • closeClose the preview window and return to the previous screen.
  • imageUpload the currently displayed single image to the digital pin badge.
  • animated_imagesGenerate a video using all the added images and audio, and upload it to the digital pin badge.
  • lapsSpecify the loop duration for the video (this can only be set when there is no audio data).
  • chevron_leftView the previous image.
  • chevron_rightView the next image.
  • downloadDownload the currently displayed image to your device as a JPEG file.
  • folder_zipDownload all the added images and audio together as a single ZIP archive.
  • deleteRemove the currently displayed image.
